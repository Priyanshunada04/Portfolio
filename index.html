01 — About

I believe the best AI interface is the one you never notice.

I work at the intersection of product design and Human-Centered AI — designing systems that surface the right information at the right moment, give the user a genuine choice, and then step back.

Rather than designing screens, I design decision architectures — the invisible scaffolding that helps people make better choices without adding cognitive load. I'm seeking a product design internship where I can apply these principles to real-world AI products at scale.
